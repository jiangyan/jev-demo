The gate

Jev returns a calibrated probability, not just a label. These thresholds turn that probability into an action. Move them and the whole queue re-decides — no further model calls, because the answers are already in.

    Is the confidence worth anything?

    Calibration is a falsifiable claim: of the answers given at 0.9, about 90% should be right. Every threshold above depends on it, so it is worth checking against human labels rather than taking on trust.
